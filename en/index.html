Method

Three habits found in every repository, which you can check by opening the code.

I validate against a published reference

Before any new calculation, I reproduce a known result: Mack (1993) for reserving, McNeil (1997) for extreme values. Then a second, independent code recomputes the output: 50 IFRS 17 files and 50 IFRS 9 loans redone in R.

I also publish what does not work

In climatXL, the effect of climate on the premium is not demonstrated, and that is the conclusion of the report. In ecl9, both models cover only 9 to 13% of the crisis losses. In IFRS 17 Copilot, an embedding fine-tuning was measured and then dropped.

The language model does not calculate

In my AI tools, every figure comes out of tested code. The model reads the documents, searches the standard, explains and writes. When the data do not allow the calculation, the tool refuses instead of returning a wrong number.
